# Research table — 50 articles

Read `02-ARTICLE-WRITING-RULES.md` and `03-TONE-AND-PERSONA.md` first. This
file is the plan for 50 articles, not the articles themselves — nothing
here gets written in full until this plan is approved, and even then,
each article is drafted on its own, checked against its own row below
before being called done.

Intro/outro device numbers refer to the numbered banks in
`03-TONE-AND-PERSONA.md`. No article's intro or outro device repeats the
immediately preceding article's. Word-count bands are a floor–ceiling
range, not a fixed number, assigned per-topic and deliberately
non-sequential across the batch.

External links: every article draws from the real authority pool —
`msme.gov.in`, `startupindia.gov.in`, `udyamregistration.gov.in`,
`sidbi.in`, `nsic.co.in`, plus RBI/GST/Income-Tax portals and recognized
business-research sources where a specific claim needs one — fetched and
confirmed live at drafting time, placed inline, never as an end list.
