    FAQ

    Honest answers to fair questions.

    Learn how to browse, read an idea and decide what to check next.

    Are these real business ideas or just inspiration?

    Each entry is a researched blueprint, not a topic suggestion. It explains what the business does day to day, who the specific customer is, how money changes hands, the realistic obstacles, and whether it suits the person building it. The point is to help you make a clear decision before you spend your time or money.

    How does pricing work?

    BBI has two paid plan prices, ₹199 and ₹299. Plan inclusions are being specified before checkout.

    What does each business blueprint cover?

    Four things come first: who specifically will pay you; how the money works; what is likely to hurt in the first year; and an honest founder-fit verdict. Sometimes that verdict is simple: do not build this one.

    How do I choose an idea?

    Start with your own situation: the time, skills, money and access you have. Then read the blueprint closely. Look for a named buyer, a believable way to earn, the first-year risks and a verdict that fits your circumstances. Do not choose an idea just because it sounds exciting.

    Can a blueprint guarantee that a business will work?

    No. A blueprint is research to help you ask better questions, not a promise of success. You still need to speak with potential customers, test whether they will pay, and check the facts in your own market. We would rather be honest about what is unknown than make a guarantee.

    What if the honest answer is not to build?

    Then the blueprint should say so. A useful research library does not push every idea. It helps you see who an idea suits, who it does not suit, and when walking away is the better decision.

    Who is this library for?

    It is for someone starting from zero — often without capital, a team or even a laptop — and for people who already earn but want to test a second-income idea without giving up what they have.

    Do I need to know what business I want before browsing?

    No. Browse by category and subcategory, explore unfamiliar fields, and compare ideas. You can start with a problem you understand or a kind of work you would like to do.

    How often is the library updated?

    New blueprints are added as research is completed. The catalogue changes over time, so treat any displayed count as a live snapshot rather than a permanent promise.

    Can I suggest a business idea for the library?

    Yes. Send the niche or sector through the Contact page. Suggestions help us understand what founders want researched next.
