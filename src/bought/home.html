The point is to help you make a clear decision before you spend your time or money.

    Four things come first: who specifically will pay you; how the money works; what is likely to hurt in the first year; and an honest founder-fit verdict. Sometimes that verdict is simple: do not build this one.

    Start with your own situation: the time, skills, money and access you have. Then read the blueprint closely. Look for a named buyer, a believable way to earn, the first-year risks and a verdict that fits your circumstances. Do not choose an idea just because it sounds exciting.
