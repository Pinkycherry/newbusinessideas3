**Keywords cross-checked 2026-10-03.** Every focus keyword below was swapped
from the single generic head term it started as (things like "AI business
ideas," "business ideas for beginners," "ecommerce business ideas" —
2-3-word phrases every large competitor already owns) to a longer, more
specific phrase that matches each article's own distinct angle instead. The
original head term stays in the additional-keywords list for topical
coverage, it just stops being the thing the page is trying to win on. A
brand-new domain cannot outrank an established site on a bare head term; it
can realistically rank on a specific long-tail phrase nobody else has
targeted with that exact framing. See `CONTENT_SCHEDULING_PLAN.md` for the
publish-date plan for this batch.
