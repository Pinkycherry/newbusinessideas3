Ethical Founder Article Plan

One file for all Ethical Founder batches (target 50). Batch 1 of 5 planned 09 Nov 2026, IST. Site: https://ethicalfounder.in/. Author: Siva Charan. Copy kept in repo folder NOV Articles- 3 website.

Site strategy and tone

Voice: Siva Charan, first person, the deal desk. A founder who reads offers, does the money maths and gives a verdict. Tone position (NN/g scales): formal 4 of 5, warm-neutral 3, bold 4, matter-of-fact 4. Grammarly tones used: assertive, formal, curious. Readers: Indian founders, small business owners and small investors. Every article: research-backed, first-person witness moments, practical checks, E-E-A-T, no cyber-security topics.

Say: specific numbers from cited reports only, plain verdicts, "I would ask", "the maths says". Avoid: hype, hedging, em dashes, negative parallelism, banned words (delve, leverage, seamless, robust, crucial, holistic), personal numbers about age or years.

Rules for every article (from 02-ARTICLE-WRITING-RULES and 03-TONE-AND-PERSONA, plus owner rules of 9 Nov 2026)

4,000 to 6,000 words, band per row. Focus keyword in H1 and 2 to 3 headings; 9 additional keywords 2 to 3 times each, counted. SEO title 60 to 80 characters, slug under 75, meta 140 to 160 characters, 5 to 7 tags ending Small Business India, keywords comma-separated. FAQ: H2, each question an H3, answer on the next line with no blank line. One line per paragraph. 5 to 10 inline external links, every one opened and live, never a link list. Intro and outro devices from the 03 banks, never back to back.

Research: Google searches in Indian regional languages only, pages 2 to 4 only, never page 1 or sponsored, last 7 days and at most 15 days, typed into Google in a visible tab, no English sites. For each article, 6 to 8 sources must be individual platforms or sites and only 2 news sites. No cyber-security topics. Rupee figures only where the cited case needs them, never forced into titles or SEO.

Images: 6 per article, each a different composition from the poster composition library (30 compositions reverse-engineered from the owner's Pinterest references), poster title and tagline that match the image, no credits, cast, dates, director or platform text. Image SEO written fresh per image, no persona line repeated.
