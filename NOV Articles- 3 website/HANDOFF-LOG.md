## Article package format (CMS one paste, note 28)

Header before H1: Title, Focus keyword, Additional keywords (comma list), Tags, SEO title, Slug, Meta description, Rank Math TOC title, Category. Then the article in Markdown from one H1.
FAQ: last H2 whose heading contains "Questions", each question a bold line ending in "?", blank line, answer paragraph. It becomes the Rank Math FAQ block.
Image section: heading "# Image prompts", then the reference-photo line, "File names:" numbered list, then for each image "Prompt N:", "Alt N:", "Title N:", "Description N:". No captions.

## Universal rules that broke before (all sites)

1. Image prompts never say "Composition 9". Each prompt writes the full layout in words: frame split, the author's action and outfit, every other person, objects, place, light, palette, poster title and tagline, camera body, lens, shutter. Only the author photo is supplied. Never the site brand name inside a prompt. Outfits change in every image. No neon, no posing, no phone or laptop staring.
2. Image SEO is SEO, never a picture description. Alt: a natural sentence with a keyword and, often, the author name. Title and description: include the brand name (Ethical Founder etc.), focus or additional keywords, author name, worded differently every time. File name: keyword-based, hyphenated. Check the image SEO log below before writing new ones.
3. Focus keyword in H1, SEO title and 2 to 3 H2 or H3 headings. Each of the 9 additional keywords used naturally 2 to 3 times in the body and, for most of them, once in a heading. No stuffing. Focus density about 0.5 to 0.8 percent.
4. Links: exactly 2 external links (opened and verified), 10 to 15 inline internal links to live posts on the same site, no source lists.
5. Research: typed Google searches in regional languages, visible tab, last 7 to 15 days, no English sites, no cyber-security topics, no loan-app pieces.
6. No em dashes, no negative parallelism, no banned words (delve, leverage, seamless, robust, crucial, holistic), no emojis, no personal numbers for the author.

## Radha Krishna approach (all UT stories, centralized)

Diwali trip or long leave visiting shops across Andhra Pradesh; never name towns. No ads, no paid promotion: he finds gaps online, then meets owners in person. Friendly and humorous, the neighbour next door, jokes, learns in real time. Never pitches; says "why can't you try something like this? The world is updating." If asked how, he explains but never gives price, quote or money talk. Talks only to the owner, never workers; asks the owner to call his kids or an educated family member or well-wisher, explains to them, gives his card and leaves. Later sends only a personal wish ("Uncle, this is Radha Krishna from UpcomingTools... Happy Diwali"), never business. Privacy: never name client shops, franchises, platforms they use, API keys or official details until the client permits. Never put invented figures in a client's mouth.
